Protective gear

  • Helmet: essential. Above 30–40 km/h a full-face helmet is the better choice.
  • Wrist guards: in a fall you instinctively put your hands out, so wrist injuries are the most common.
  • Knee and elbow guards: especially while learning and at higher speeds.
  • Shoes: closed, with a firm sole, so your foot doesn't slip off the pedal.
  • Body armour and padded shorts: for higher speeds and long rides.

On the road

  • Ride at less than 80% of the wheel's top speed. That leaves power in reserve.
  • If the wheel beeps or tilts its front back, slow down straight away.
  • Take care on potholes, tram or railway tracks, kerbs and wet road markings.
  • Always give way to pedestrians.
  • At night, switch the lights on and wear something reflective.
  • Don't play music so loud in your headphones that you can't hear what's around you.

Regular checks: check the tyre pressure, bolts, pedals and battery condition regularly.
