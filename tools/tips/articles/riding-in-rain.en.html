What an IP rating means

An IP rating has two digits: the first shows protection against dust, the second against water. An "X" means that part wasn't tested.

  • IPX6: protection against powerful water jets, which covers rain and splashes.
  • IPX6 does not mean the wheel can survive being submerged.

In our catalogue, the Aero, Aeon, Xeno, Apex and Sherman-P are rated IPX6. The manufacturer doesn't list an IP rating for the Patton-S or the Lynx-S.

In the rain

  • Wet roads give less grip and longer braking distances. Slow down and avoid sudden manoeuvres.
  • Road markings, metal covers, tracks and wet leaves are especially slippery.
  • Avoid deep puddles: you can't tell how deep they are or what's underneath.

After the rain

  • Wipe the wheel down and let it dry.
  • Don't charge it wet. Wait until the charging port is completely dry.
  • Don't use a pressure washer. A damp cloth is enough.
  • Keep the charging port cover closed at all times.
