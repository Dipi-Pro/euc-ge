Charging

  • For everyday riding, charging to 80–90% is enough. Charge to 100% before a long ride and at least once a month, so the battery management system (BMS) can balance the cells.
  • Don't run it flat. On a low battery the wheel has less power in reserve, so below 20% ride gently: no hard acceleration, no steep climbs.
  • The battery is warm after a ride. Let it rest for 15–20 minutes before charging.
  • Use the original charger or a trusted one with the correct voltage.
  • Charge on a dry, solid surface away from anything flammable, and don't leave it charging unattended overnight.

Winter

  • Range drops in the cold. That's normal and doesn't mean the battery is damaged.
  • Don't charge a cold battery. If the wheel has been outside below 0°C, bring it indoors for 1–2 hours first.
  • Storing it for weeks or months: leave it at 50–60%, switched off, indoors in a dry place (10–25°C). Check it once a month and top it up if needed.
  • Don't store it fully charged or completely empty.

When to contact us: if the battery drains faster than usual, charging stalls at one level, or the shell gets unusually hot, smells or swells. In the last three cases, stop using it straight away.
