Getting ready

  • Put on protective gear: a helmet, wrist guards and knee guards.
  • Pick a flat, quiet spot with a wall or fence, or a friend who can hold your hand.
  • In the app, set a low speed limit and, if your model has one, a soft riding mode.

How to start

  1. Put one foot on a pedal, keep the other on the ground and lean a hand on the wall.
  2. Carefully bring the other foot up and move along the wall.
  3. Look ahead, not at your feet. Bend your knees slightly and keep your body relaxed.
  4. Shift your weight forward to speed up, and back to slow down.
  5. To turn, rotate your shoulders and head towards the turn and your body will follow.
  6. To stop, slow down and put one foot on the ground.

Tips

  • Practise in short sessions (20–30 minutes). You fall more often when you're tired.
  • If the wheel beeps or tilts its front back, you're at the limit: slow down straight away.
  • For the first few weeks, avoid busy streets and traffic.
