Checking

  • Check the pressure before every ride, and at least once a week, with a digital gauge. Pressure drops in the cold, so check more often in winter.
  • Start from the manufacturer's recommendation. In most cases that's 30–37 PSI (about 2.1–2.55 bar).
  • Then adjust for your weight: a heavier rider needs more pressure. Change it in small steps.

More or less?

  • Higher pressure: longer range and lighter handling, but you feel potholes more sharply and grip is lower.
  • Lower pressure: more comfort and grip, but shorter range, heavier handling and a higher risk of damaging the tyre or rim.

When to replace it

  • the tread is worn down or smooth;
  • cracks or bulges appear on the sidewall;
  • it keeps getting punctures.

Replacement: changing a unicycle tyre often means taking the shell and motor apart, so it's best done at a service centre. At the ZIPI.GE service centre we can also help you choose between street and off-road tyres.
